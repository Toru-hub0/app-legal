Support

Frequently asked questions and how to reach us.

Do these apps collect my personal data?

No. Our apps do not collect, store, or share any personal information or usage data. See the Privacy Policy below for details.

Is my data backed up or synced?

Any content you create stays on your device only. We do not have access to it, so it is not synced to our servers. Use your device’s iCloud or local backup to preserve your data.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Please use the contact option below. Include your device model, iOS version, and the name of the app so we can help you faster.

The app isn’t working correctly. What should I try first?

Try fully closing and reopening the app, restarting your device, and making sure you are running the latest version from the App Store. If the issue continues, contact us below.

Contact Us

Have a question or need help? Email us and we’ll get back to you as soon as possible.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications (the “Apps”) published by Toru Kitajima (“we”, “us”, or “our”) on the Apple App Store. By using any of our Apps, you agree to the terms described below.

1 No Data Collection

Our Apps do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data. We have no servers that receive information from the Apps, and we do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.

2 Information Stored on Your Device

Any data you create within an App (such as settings or content) is stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to this data. If you delete an App, its locally stored data is removed along with it.

3 Third-Party Services

Our Apps do not integrate any third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data, including analytics, crash reporting, or advertising services.

4 Children’s Privacy

Because our Apps do not collect any data, they do not knowingly collect information from children of any age. The Apps are safe to use by all audiences in this respect.

5 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of our Apps after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

6 Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email us at tuna.toko@gmail.com.
