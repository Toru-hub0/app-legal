Support

Frequently asked questions and how to reach us.

Do these apps collect my personal data?

Most of our apps collect nothing at all — they run entirely on your device. Some apps offer optional online or multiplayer features; when you use those, a small amount of session data (an anonymous ID, a display name you choose, and your in-game actions) is sent to our backend so the feature can work. Apps also check our update server for updated app content, which sends a random installation identifier and, after a crash, a short error description. We never collect your real name, email, or location. See the Privacy Policy below for details.

Is my data backed up or synced?

Content you create offline stays on your device only and is not synced to our servers. When you use an app’s online or multiplayer features, the data needed for that shared session is sent to our backend so other players can take part; it is used only to run the session. Use your device’s iCloud or local backup to preserve on-device data.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Please use the contact option below. Include your device model, iOS version, and the name of the app so we can help you faster.

The app isn’t working correctly. What should I try first?

Try fully closing and reopening the app, restarting your device, and making sure you are running the latest version from the App Store. If the issue continues, contact us below.

Contact Us

Have a question or need help? Email us and we’ll get back to you as soon as possible.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications (the “Apps”) published by Toru Kitajima (“we”, “us”, or “our”) on the Apple App Store. By using any of our Apps, you agree to the terms described below.

1 Data Collection

Most of our Apps do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data. They run entirely on your device, with no analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies. Some Apps offer optional online or multiplayer features; the limited data those features require is described in section 3 below. Apps that receive content updates also contact our update server as described in section 4. When used offline, our Apps transmit nothing.

2 Information Stored on Your Device

Any data you create within an App (such as settings or content) is stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to this data. If you delete an App, its locally stored data is removed along with it.

3 Online & Multiplayer Features

Some of our Apps include optional online or multiplayer features (for example, playing a game with other people in real time). These features are only active when you choose to use them. When you do, the App creates an anonymous identifier so the server can tell players apart, and it sends the information needed to run the shared session — such as a display name you enter and your in-game actions — to our backend provider. We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. This information is used solely to operate the live session and is never used for analytics, advertising, or profiling. If you only use an App offline or in single-player mode, no information leaves your device.

4 Automatic Content Updates

When an App starts, it may check our update server (Expo Updates, u.expo.dev) for updated app content. This check sends a randomly generated installation identifier (created on your device and not tied to your identity or hardware) and basic technical information such as the app version and platform. If the previous session ended in a crash, a short error description may also be sent to help us fix stability issues. This information is used only to deliver updates and improve reliability — never for advertising or tracking — and is not shared with third parties.

5 Third-Party Services

Our Apps do not integrate third-party SDKs for analytics or advertising. The optional online features described in section 3 are powered by Supabase, a backend hosting provider that stores and relays session data on our behalf. Supabase processes only the data needed to run those features and does not use it for advertising or tracking.

6 Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Apps that collect no data are safe for all audiences in this respect, and the optional online features described above are not directed to children and do not gather any real-world identity.

7 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of our Apps after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8 Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email us at tuna.toko@gmail.com.
